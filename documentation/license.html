Credits

Ruzor exists because of the Pyzor project and its published documentation. This documentation is adapted for Ruzor and cross-checked against the upstream Pyzor 1.1 documentation at pyzor.org.

Pyzor began as an open implementation inspired by Razor-style collaborative spam detection. The public Pyzor service documented by upstream is public.pyzor.org:24441; the Ruzor public endpoint documented here is public.ruzor.org:24441.
